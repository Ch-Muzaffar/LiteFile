Questions

Frequently asked questions

How many PDFs can I merge at once?

There is no hard limit set by LiteFile. In practice, merging very large files (combined total over 200 MB) may be slow on older devices since all processing happens in your browser. For typical document PDFs, merging 10–20 files works smoothly.

Can I reorder the PDFs before merging?

Yes. After adding your files, drag them into the order you want before clicking Merge. The merged PDF will follow the exact order shown in the list — the first file at the top becomes the first pages of the merged document.

Will hyperlinks and bookmarks be preserved?

Internal hyperlinks within each PDF are preserved. Bookmarks and table-of-contents entries from the original files are not merged — only page content and internal links are copied to the combined document.

Is the merged PDF identical in quality to the originals?

Yes. LiteFile copies pages directly without re-rendering or re-compressing them. Text stays crisp and selectable, images retain their original quality, and the file size of the merged PDF is roughly the combined size of the originals.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

No. Password-protected PDFs cannot be merged. Remove the password protection from each file first, then upload them to LiteFile. Use Split PDF if you need to extract specific pages before merging.
