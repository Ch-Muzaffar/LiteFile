Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I split a PDF into individual pages?

Upload your PDF, select All pages mode, and click Split. Each page is extracted into its own PDF file and all files are bundled into a ZIP archive for download.

Everything runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded to a server.

Can I extract just a specific range of pages?

Yes. Choose Page range mode, enter the start and end page numbers, and click Extract pages. The result is a single PDF containing only those pages — useful for pulling out a chapter, a section, or a single page.

How do I split a PDF into multiple parts with different page ranges?

Use Custom ranges mode. Enter comma-separated ranges — for example 1-3, 5, 7-10 — and click Split.

This creates three PDFs: one for pages 1–3, one for page 5, and one for pages 7–10. They are downloaded together as a ZIP archive.

Is the output quality the same as the original?

Yes. Pages are copied directly from the original PDF without re-rendering or re-compressing. Text stays crisp and selectable, images keep their original quality, and the file size of each output PDF matches its share of the original.

Can I split a password-protected PDF?

No. Password-protected PDFs cannot be split. Remove the password protection from the file first — many PDF viewers let you do this via Print → Save as PDF — then upload the unprotected copy to LiteFile.
