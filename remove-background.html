Questions

Frequently asked questions

How does it work?

LiteFile uses an AI background removal model. Your image is processed and the result — a transparent PNG — is returned within seconds.

Can I apply a plain background color?

Yes. After the background is removed, a color picker appears below the before/after comparison. Choose white, black, blue, red, green, or any custom color — the downloaded PNG will have that plain color as its background.

This is especially useful for ID photos, passport photos, and e-commerce product images that must be on a specific background color.

How accurate is the background removal?

The AI delivers fast, high-quality background removal suitable for people, products, animals, and objects against clear backgrounds.

Complex or very busy backgrounds may have imperfect edges around fine details like hair and fur. For most everyday use cases — product photos, portraits, logos — results are clean and ready to use without further editing.

What format is the output file?

The output is a transparent PNG. PNG is the only widely supported format that preserves full transparency, so your subject is saved with a clean cutout — no background, just the subject.

You can place the PNG directly on any background in Canva, Figma, Photoshop, PowerPoint, or any other tool that supports transparent images.

Can I use this for product photos?

Yes — product photography on plain or white backgrounds is where this tool performs best. The model is designed for clean subject isolation, making it ideal for e-commerce listings, portfolio images, and marketing assets.

After removing the background, use Compress Image to reduce the PNG file size, or Resize Image to get exact pixel dimensions for your platform.
