FAQ

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. LiteFile runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly. Your files never leave your device — nothing is uploaded, stored, or transmitted to any server at any point.

Is LiteFile really free to use?

Yes, completely free. There are no hidden costs, no signup required, and no watermarks on any output files. LiteFile is supported by optional donations and may show non-intrusive ads in the future.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. All tools are fully responsive and work on modern iOS and Android browsers. The background remover requires downloading a 176MB AI model on first use — we recommend doing that on Wi-Fi.
